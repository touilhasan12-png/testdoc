# Pilot credits and provenance

## Maps and history

* NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, *Draining the Oceans*, ID 3487: Blue Marble Next Generation July 4K map and matching land/sea mask; the pilot uses a locally graded derivative. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3487/
* Natural Earth: 1:50m admin-0 polygons used in BlenderGIS; world-atlas's 1:110m Natural Earth-derived country polygons for the globe. Natural Earth is public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ ; https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
* A. Ourednik and contributors, Historical Basemaps, `world_1500.geojson`: Iberian historical-realm overlay, approximate, not an exact 1519 sovereignty reconstruction. GPL-3.0; original data and license are in `data/`. https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps
* Background checks: https://origins.osu.edu/milestones/magellan-circumnavigation-earth ; https://artsandculture.google.com/story/magellan-s-expedition-and-elcano-s-first-circumnavigation-of-the-globe-fundacion-elkano/-gVBFYTKUG3YLg?hl=en ; https://www.britannica.com/place/Strait-of-Magellan
* Geography and route are illustrative modern maps with approximate waypoints. The low-resolution globe does not resolve the narrow strait. No route is suitable for navigation.

## Artwork and music

* The ship, navigator, and ocean source images are AI-generated specifically for this project. The navigator is a fictionalized interpretation, not an authenticated likeness. The ship cutout was segmented with rembg; a separate lower-detail mesh was reconstructed with TripoSR.
* English narration and musical bed are the existing project-provided audio; no reference-video footage or audio was copied.
* The Castilian/Burgundian banner is an illustrative historical repaint of the `flag-icons` Spain field template; the modern flag is not depicted as a 1519 flag. flag-icons: MIT, https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons.
* Fonts: Anton and Lato under the SIL Open Font License. License texts accompany the font files.
* Remotion is subject to its license; eligibility depends on the user's circumstances: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq
