## 6. Real satellite base — source, licence, resolution

- **Imagery**: GSI Japan "seamlessphoto" XYZ tiles, `https://cyberjapandata.gsi.go.jp/xyz/seamlessphoto/{z}/{x}/{y}.jpg` (low zoom = satellite mosaic, high zoom = aerial orthophoto). © 国土地理院 / Geospatial Information Authority of Japan; use under the Government of Japan Standard Terms of Use (compatible with CC BY 4.0), credit shown on screen on every frame.
- **No-data fill**: GSI has no imagery over much of the Pacific at low zoom (flat RGB 53,53,53). Only those pixels are replaced with NASA EOSDIS GIBS WMTS `BlueMarble_NextGeneration` (GoogleMapsCompatible_Level8, open NASA data, credit on screen) by `scripts/merge_basemap.py`; 621 tiles touched (`src/data/gibs-filled.txt`).
- **Resolution**: 256×256 px tiles, z2–z17 (≈ 150 km/px at z2 … ≈ 1 m/px at z17 near Tokyo). Tiles are downloaded **once** by `scripts/fetch_assets.py` for exactly the camera paths, re-encoded (JPEG q55) and committed in `public/tiles/` (manifest `src/data/tile-manifest.json`) — **no network access at render time**.
- **Boundaries/overlays**: geoBoundaries gbOpen JPN ADM1 (ODbL) and ADM2 (CC BY-SA 2.0), derived from OpenStreetMap (Wambacher), release commit `9469f09`; simplified with shapely (`scripts/build_geo.py`). Credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors via geoBoundaries" on screen.
- Overlays (borders, arrows, rings, numbers) are programmed above the imagery; the ground itself is real imagery everywhere.
